## 👋 Hey there!
I'm a Computer Engineering student at the University of Zaragoza with a builder's mindset, passionate about low-level systems, processor architecture, HPC and open infrastructure.
 
Right now I'm working on hardware prefetching ([Berti](https://doi.org/10.1109/MICRO56248.2022.00072)) for my thesis, and on porting [ILVES](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01376), a molecular dynamics constraint solver, to GPU with CUDA. Last summer I was a Summer Student at CERN, writing a Rust–C++ logging bridge for the LHC Quench Protection System ([report](https://repository.cern/records/0r2at-p8j04)). I also maintain [spaceship-prompt](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/spaceship-prompt) in the AUR.
